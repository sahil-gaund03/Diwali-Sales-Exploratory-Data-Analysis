# Diwali-Sales-Exploratory-Data-Analysis
Diwali Sales Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
This project performs a comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on Diwali sales data to improve customer experience and drive business growth. By analyzing purchasing patterns, the project identifies key demographics and consumer behaviors to help businesses optimize their marketing strategies.

🚀 Project Objectives
The primary goal is to analyze the sales data and uncover insights regarding:

Demographics: Who are the primary buyers in terms of gender and age group?

Geography: Which states contribute most to the total number of orders and revenue?

Occupations: Which professional sectors have the highest purchasing power?

Product Categories: Which categories and specific products are most popular?

📊 Key Insights
Gender: Women constitute the majority of buyers and have higher total purchasing power than men.

Age: The most active consumer base is the 26-35 years age group.

State: Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka are the top three states by both order volume and total sales value.

Occupation: Individuals working in IT, Healthcare, and Aviation sectors are the most frequent shoppers.

Product: The Food, Clothing, and Electronics categories show the highest demand.

The project uses Seaborn and Matplotlib to create various charts:

Bar Charts: Comparing sales across different states and occupations.
